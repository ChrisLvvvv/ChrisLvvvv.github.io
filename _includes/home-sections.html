About

I am an M.S.E. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania, focused on computer architecture and digital IC design. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, with a minor in Economics.

My background spans processor microarchitecture, RTL design, cache and memory systems, and hardware-aware AI systems. I have designed and verified RISC-V processors in SystemVerilog, including pipelined and out-of-order architectures with register renaming, reorder buffers, reservation stations, load/store queues, and cache coherence.

My research has also focused on GPU-based AI inference, including KV-cache efficiency, scheduling behavior, and vLLM/PagedAttention workloads. At Penn, I am continuing to work across computer architecture, SoC design, digital IC design, and hardware/software co-design, with particular interest in CPU/GPU architectures and AI accelerators.
