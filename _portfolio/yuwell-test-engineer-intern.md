I worked as a Test Engineer Intern at Yuwell Global in Danyang, Jiangsu, China, from April to August 2022. Under engineer supervision, I operated EMI/EMS test equipment and performed ESD and surge testing for medical devices.

I followed prescribed test procedures and recorded the results. This role focused on supervised test execution and documentation, and is separate from my hardware-engineering internship in 2023.
