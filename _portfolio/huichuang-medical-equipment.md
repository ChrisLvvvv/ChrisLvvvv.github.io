## Role and platform

I worked as a Research and Development Intern at Huichuang Medical Equipment in Beijing from February to August 2025. My work supported an existing transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) device platform through circuit migration, assigned revisions, and technical documentation.

## Schematic and PCB migration

I migrated schematics and multilayer PCB layouts from KiCad to Altium. The work covered control, power, and cooling-module boards and included assigned circuit revisions and layout adjustments. This was migration and development within an existing platform, rather than an independent design of the entire TMS product.

During circuit review, I proposed replacing discrete circuitry with an integrated I2C-controlled IC. I presented the approach in team design meetings, and the team adopted the proposal.

## Internal engineering reference

I independently wrote a Chinese technical manual of approximately 30,000 characters. It documents circuit analysis, calibration, operating procedures, and test workflows based on my own analysis and hands-on testing. The completed manual was archived as an internal technical reference.
