## Hardware engineering internship

I worked as a Hardware Engineer Intern at Yuwell Global in Danyang, Jiangsu, China, from May to August 2023. Under engineer guidance, I supported assembly and testing of infrared thermometer boards using Heimann sensor modules. My work included SMT soldering and oscilloscope-based electrical measurements.

I also assisted with board-level debugging of returned Japanese-market units. I checked for short circuits and physical board faults, performed soldering rework where appropriate, and escalated unresolved issues to engineers.

This internship is separate from my earlier test-engineering role at Yuwell in 2022.
