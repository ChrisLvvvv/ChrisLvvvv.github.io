In this course, our team followed the complete engineering design cycle to identify a user-centered problem, generate feasible concepts, build early prototypes, and validate the solution through testing and feedback. The project emphasized structured creativity, engineering communication, and iterative refinement based on user needs and performance data.

## My Contribution

- Conducted early-stage problem definition and user interviews to refine design requirements  
- Created initial sketches, engineering models, and low-fidelity prototypes  
- Led technical evaluation of concept feasibility and materials selection  
- Built functional prototypes and performed iterative testing with documented improvements  
- Contributed to final report, design review presentation, and project documentation  

## Technical Summary

- **Concepts:** Design thinking, user research, functional decomposition, prototyping  
- **Methods:** Requirement analysis, concept screening, iterative refinement  
- **Tools:** CAD modeling, rapid prototyping tools, structured design templates  

## Outcome

Delivered a fully documented engineering design project demonstrating systematic innovation, clear engineering justification, and the ability to translate user needs into practical design decisions. The work reflects strong competency in collaborative engineering and structured problem solving.
