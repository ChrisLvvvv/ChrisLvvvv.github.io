## Capstone scope

CoMET was a Corning-sponsored capstone project at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in Spring 2026. I built and integrated the physical-device prototype for a meeting assistant. My contribution covered hardware and device-control software, with connectivity to the team's backend processing system.

## Device integration

I integrated a Raspberry Pi 4 running Linux with a ReSpeaker USB microphone array, a USB speaker, local storage, and network connectivity. The device captured meeting audio locally and transferred recordings into the team's processing workflow.

I designed and wired GPIO button and LED circuits and implemented the device software for recording start/stop, microphone mute, and status indication. These physical controls let users operate the prototype and see its current state.

## End-to-end device checks

I validated audio capture during multi-speaker team meetings. I also tested local audio storage, transfer to the backend, button operation, and the corresponding LED state changes.

The backend and AI meeting-processing functionality were team systems connected to the device. This project describes my hardware and device-integration work rather than claiming implementation of those services.
